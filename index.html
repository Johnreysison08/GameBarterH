Frequently asked
Is GameBarterHub free to use?

Yes. Creating an account and posting trades doesn't cost anything.

How do I make an offer?

Open any available listing and tap Offer. The owner gets a notification and can accept or decline.

How do I stay safe while trading?

Keep your conversations and offers inside GameBarterHub, never share your password with anyone, and check a trader's profile before you commit. If something looks wrong, open a support ticket from the chat button.

What can I trade?

In-game items, accounts, and resources across the games listed on the site. Pick the matching game when you post.
